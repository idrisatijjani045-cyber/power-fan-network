# Power Fan Network Mobile Application

Power Fan Network wata kyakkyawar manhaja ce ta Flutter da aka gina domin sarrafa tsarin hakar coin (FAN Mining), gudanar da ayyukan kafafan sada zumunta (Social Media Tasks), da kuma kiyaye asusun mambobi.

## 🚀 Muhimman Fasaloli (Key Features)

* **Real-Time Mining Engine:** Lissafin FAN balance yana ƙaruwa a kowane sakan tare da amfani da amintaccen lokacin Firebase.
* **4-Decimal Precision:** Nunin balance ɗin FAN tare da lambobi 4 bayan digo (misali: `20.0000 FAN`).
* **Social Media Rewards Section:** Ayyuka na musamman na X (Twitter), TikTok, Facebook, Telegram, da Instagram tare da lada na **+2 FAN** kowace guda.
* **Strict Claim Requirements:** Maballin CLAIM yana aiki ne kawai idan an kammala sa'o'i 24 na mining da kuma dukkan ayyukan sada zumunta.
* **Modern Purple UI:** Kyakkyawan tsarin shafi mai launin violet, gold accent, da zane na musamman na miner character da katin aiki.

## 🛠️ Kayan Aiki (Tech Stack)

* **Framework:** Flutter (Dart)
* **Backend:** Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database)
* **Build System:** GitHub Actions (Android Release APK)
